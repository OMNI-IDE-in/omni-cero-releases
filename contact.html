Getting Omni Cero & your license

From download to a licensed OR workstation in three steps — everything runs offline once installed.

1 · Download the installer

Live C-arm + Cero (~195 MB) or XVR + Cero Integrated (~319 MB) — self-contained offline installers for Windows 10/11 64-bit. No runtime prerequisites.

Choose your edition →

2 · Install on your workstation

Run the installer on the machine that will live in the imaging room. C-arm capture is driver-free on Windows, and nothing is sent to the cloud.

See hardware & C-arm compatibility →

3 · Request your license

Write to us with your workstation details and we will issue a node-locked license — bound to the motherboard, CPU and system UUID, signed and verified fully offline.

Request a license →
