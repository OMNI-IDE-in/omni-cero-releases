We believe advanced intraoperative imaging and surgical navigation should begin with the equipment already in your operating room. Omni Cero turns that conviction into resilient, offline medical workstation software.

The Core Origin

All Possibilities.
Zero Compromises.

Omni Cero takes its name from Latin omni ("all") and Spanish cero ("zero"). Together, they express our design brief: unite the entire imaging suite — from flat-panel digital radiography (DROC) to live C-arm streaming and 3D navigation — while demanding zero cloud dependency, zero telemetry, and zero dropped frames.

Operating suites cannot afford to pause because an external cloud activation server is unreachable or hospital Wi-Fi is intermittent. Omni Cero computes every algorithm, from Digital Subtraction Angiography to 6-DOF neural pose registration, right on the workstation in the room.

Engineered in Bengaluru, India by Omni IDE, our platform is designed for orthopedic, spine, and vascular surgeons who demand sub-millimeter precision from standard clinical hardware.

Security & Licensing

Cryptographically Node-Locked.
100% Offline.

Omni Cero verifies workstation licenses mathematically on-device using asymmetric Ed25519 cryptographic signatures. Each license binds directly to the workstation's motherboard, CPU, and system hardware UUID.

Zero Cloud Activation

No phone-home calls, no periodic re-activation pings. Once installed, it operates indefinitely offline.

Hospital Data Sovereignty

Patient DICOM studies, live fluoroscopy cine runs, and 3D CT volumes never leave your hospital LAN.

AVX2 CPU Optimized

High-throughput neural pose solver runs on standard Intel/AMD CPUs without requiring dedicated GPU hardware.
