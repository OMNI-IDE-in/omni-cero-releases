Three clinical applications

One workstation, three specialized tools

Purpose-built applications sharing a single runtime — replacing the fragmented viewer, capture, and planning software found in most imaging rooms.

Live C-Arm Video Station

Real-time 30 FPS fluoroscopy from any surgical C-arm via plug-and-play USB HDMI/RCA capture. No drivers, no configuration.

  • Sub-frame latency display with instant spacebar last-image hold
  • Automatic 16-bit DICOM Part 10 conversion with full metadata
  • One-click batch import of existing Hold.bmp sequences
OmniCero_LiveCarm.exe

3D XVR Surgical Navigation

Fuses preoperative CT/MRI volumes with intraoperative 2D fluoroscopy for real-time 6-DOF guidance during orthopedic and spine procedures.

  • Deep anatomical pose solver with neural 2D/3D registration
  • Sub-millimeter target accuracy under 0.8 mm MTRE
  • Automatic LAO/RAO and cranial/caudal gantry angle readout
OmniCero_XVR_Navigation.exe

Intraoperative PACS & 3D MPR

Native medical viewer with multi-planar reconstruction and an embedded DICOM C-STORE archive — query, retrieve, and store over LAN.

  • Interactive axial, coronal, and sagittal re-slicing
  • Cinematic 3D volume rendering with Hounsfield presets
  • Jump from a live exposure directly into the 3D volume
OmniCero.exe
Security & licensing

Cryptographically node-locked. Fully offline.

Operating rooms can't depend on the cloud. Omni Cero verifies licenses mathematically on-device using Ed25519 signatures — patient data never leaves the hospital.

Hardware-bound activation

Licenses bind to motherboard, CPU, and system UUID — they can't be copied to another machine.

Zero telemetry

No phone-home, no analytics, no cloud dependency of any kind.

Isolated signing key

The private signing key never ships with the product — installations carry only the public key.
