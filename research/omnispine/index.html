Real-World C-Arm Physics

Five Hospital Barriers Solved
Purely in Software

Standard mobile C-arms were never built for Axial CT. OmniSpine™ solves the five physical distortions in software — replacing INR 2.5–4 Crore imported 3D gantries.

Barrier 01

Non-Isocentric Orbit & Mechanical Gravity Sag (±6 to ±10 mm)

Open mobile C-arms flex under gravity during orbital rotation. OmniSpine™ locks each projection to sub-pixel calibrated 3D geometry so rays converge sharply on true cortical bone boundaries.

Barrier 02

Automatic Exposure Control (AEC) & Truncated Lateral Torsos

As a C-arm sweeps from AP to Lateral, exposure jumps dynamically while the patient's abdomen fills the detector. OmniSpine™ tracks true beam reference without mistaking soft tissue for air.

Barrier 03

Edge-On Operating Table Rail Photon Starvation

When a C-arm shoots edge-on through dense OT table rails, detector counts drop near zero. OmniSpine™ automatically detects and heals photon-starved projections (119,621 pixels healed on real Siemens spins).

Barrier 04

Real Physical Compton Scatter & Polychromatic Beam Hardening

Wide cone-beam geometry generates heavy scatter haze and energy hardening through dense vertebrae. OmniSpine™ performs physics-based scatter suppression and linearization before Axial formation.

Barrier 05

Limited-Angle Table Clearance (120°–165° Arcs)

When OT table rails restrict orbital sweeps below the 190°–200° short-scan threshold, OmniSpine™ automatically engages an edge-preserving solver that closes missing cortical walls without AI hallucination.
