9-DOF Physics & Geometry

Four Physical Barriers Solved
for Mobile C-Arms

Conventional circular CBCT blurs spine anatomy on open C-arm frames. Our engine resolves the underlying physical distortions in software — with zero hardware modifications.

Barrier 01

Gravity-Induced Mechanical Flex (Up to ±8.5 mm)

Unlike closed CT rings, open mobile C-arms bend dynamically under gravity during orbital spin. Our 9-DOF multi-axis compensation eliminates double-contour bone smearing.

Barrier 02

Non-360° Short-Scan Redundancy Shading

Surgical tables restrict C-arm arcs to ~198°. Standard backprojection double-counts redundant rays, creating shading artifacts that our engine resolves cleanly.

Barrier 03

Lateral Torso & Abdomen Truncation

Patient shoulders, ribcages, and hips extend wider than flat-panel detectors. Uncompensated truncation induces cupping bias and degrades bone contrast.

Barrier 04

Titanium Pedicle Screw Starbursts

Dense metal implants project dark starburst bands directly across the spinal canal. OmniCBCT restores canal visibility so surgeons can confirm pedicle wall integrity.
